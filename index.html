About Us

Rescue Connect is a real-time disaster coordination platform designed to save lives and streamline recovery efforts. Our mission is to bring together volunteers, authorities, and affected citizens through one centralized, intelligent system.

Using AI-driven disaster prediction models, instant alert systems, and real-time resource mapping, Rescue Connect ensures that help reaches where it's needed most—fast. We also provide mental health support and connect victims with virtual counseling professionals during and after disasters.

We believe that technology and community spirit combined can overcome any emergency. With transparency, collaboration, and accessibility at the core, Rescue Connect aims to redefine how disaster relief is managed.
